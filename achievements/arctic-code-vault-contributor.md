## What it was

On 2 February 2020, GitHub took a snapshot of every **active public repository** (roughly, repos with at least one commit in the year before the snapshot, plus repos with enough stars). The data was written to archival film reels designed to last 1,000 years and stored in a decommissioned coal mine deep in the Arctic permafrost.

Anyone who had authored commits in those repositories received the badge.

## Can I still get it?

No. The snapshot was a one-time event. GitHub says future archives will be part of the ongoing GitHub Archive Program, but no new badge has been tied to them.
