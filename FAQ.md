# ❓ FAQ

<details open>
<summary><b>I did the thing. Why don't I have the badge yet?</b></summary>

Achievements are processed in the background. Most show up within minutes, but sometimes it takes **a few hours**. Refresh `github.com/<you>?tab=achievements`.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do private repositories count?</b></summary>

Only if you turn on **Settings → Public profile → Contributions & activity → Include private contributions on my profile**. Links to private events are only shown to people who already have access to that repo.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I hide my achievements?</b></summary>

Yes. In **Settings → Public profile**, uncheck **Show Achievements on my profile**. The badges stay on your account and come back if you turn the setting on again.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I lose an achievement?</b></summary>

Generally no. You keep it if a starred repo loses stars or you cancel a sponsorship. GitHub can remove achievements gained by abuse, such as fake stars or spam PRs.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do the x2, x3, x4 labels come from?</b></summary>

They show the tier: **x2 = Bronze, x3 = Silver, x4 = Gold**. The ring around the badge changes color to match.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do organizations get achievements?</b></summary>

No. Achievements are for **personal accounts** only. Stars or sponsorships that belong to an organization don't count for any member.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is this artwork official?</b></summary>

No. Every badge in this repository is original vector art generated by [`scripts/generate_badges.py`](scripts/generate_badges.py), made in the spirit of the real badges. Your profile shows GitHub's own artwork.
</details>
